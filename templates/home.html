Personalised Treatment Query

Ask one treatment-related question about a patient. The system extracts a structured profile, checks GenomicsDB and Variantscape for any gene/variant mentioned, then searches LiteratureDB (papers and clinical guidelines) and returns a synthesised answer.

This is a research demonstration tool only — do not enter real patient data. Use fictional or exemplary patient characteristics only. Inputs are sent to a third-party LLM service and cached for research evaluation. Please also note that the literature database is currently only retrieving relevant literature for genitourinary cancers.
